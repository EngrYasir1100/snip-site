Privacy Policy

Snip: Subscription Tracker · Last updated: 3 November 2026

The short version: the subscriptions you track in Snip are stored only on your phone. We don't have accounts, servers, or a database of your data. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier only if you allow it.

1. Data you enter

Subscription names, prices, dates, categories, notes and settings are saved locally on your device. They are never uploaded to us. If you delete the app, or use Settings › Delete all my data, they are permanently removed.

2. Reminders

Renewal and trial reminders are scheduled locally by your device's operating system. No reminder content leaves your phone.

3. Third-party services

4. Tracking & consent

On iOS, Snip asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any advertising identifier is used. If you decline, you'll only see non-personalised ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, a consent form (Google UMP) appears before ads load, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. Snip Pro removes all ads.

5. Children

Snip is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

6. Your rights

Because we don't hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side. Your data lives on your device and is under your control. For data held by Google or RevenueCat, use the links above.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
