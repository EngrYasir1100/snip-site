FAQ

How do I cancel Snip Pro?

On iPhone, go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Snip › Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid being charged again. On Android, open the Play Store › your profile picture › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Snip › Cancel subscription, or go to play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

I bought Pro but it isn't active

Open Snip › Settings › Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID (iPhone) or Google account (Android) you used to buy it.

Does Snip see my bank account?

No. Snip never connects to your bank. You add subscriptions yourself, and they are stored only on your phone.

I'm not getting reminders

Open Snip › Settings › Notification permission and allow notifications. Reminders fire at 9:00 AM local time.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. On iPhone, request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, see Google Play's refund page.
